How a workshop runs

  1. We collect the open questions of the team.
  2. Every question gets an owner and a date.
    • Small ones are decided on the spot.
    • Big ones get a proposal first.
  3. The results land in your docs the same day.

Tip: bring the people who have to live with the decisions, not only the ones who make them.
